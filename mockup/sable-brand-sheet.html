Three rising plumes and a Garamond wordmark. Deep Berkeley navy, warm paper, one sky-blue accent, used sparingly enough that it always means something.

01

The mark

Plume
Width, each plume1 u
Heights1.7 u · 2.3 u · 2.9 u
Gap0.3 u
Corner60% · 60% · 60% · 0

Each plume is a teardrop with three rounded corners and one square heel, all seated on a shared baseline. Heights step evenly; tone steps with them, so the eye lands on the tallest, darkest form.
