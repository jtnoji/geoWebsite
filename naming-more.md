# More names

Seventy-odd, none repeated from v1, v2 or v3. Grouped by where they come from, so
you can react to a seam rather than a list. Starred are the ones I would defend in
a room.

Domains: the v3 footnote stands. Nothing here is free, pick on merit, I will chase
prices once you point at two or three.

## Bibliography and reference

The seam I would mine first. These are all words about whose name appears where,
which is the product with no describing required.

- ★ **Concordance** — an index of every place a word appears in a body of text. Your
  report, given its proper name four hundred years early.
- ★ **Provenance** — where a thing came from and whose hands it passed through. The
  cited-sources half of the audit, exactly.
- ★ **Imprint** — the publisher's name on the spine. Also what a thing leaves on
  memory.
- **Colophon** — carried over from v3 because it belongs to this seam.
- **Masthead** — the panel that names who is responsible.
- **Marginalia** — the notes a reader leaves beside the text.
- **Lemma** — the canonical form of a word, the one the dictionary lists.
- **Gloss** — the note that explains a term. Also surface shine, usefully double.
- **Errata** — the page admitting what was wrong. Very on-brand for a company whose
  differentiator is honesty.
- **Recto** · **Folio** · **Frontispiece** · **Dateline**

## Grammar and rhetoric

- ★ **Referent** — the thing a name actually points to. Quietly perfect and almost
  nobody has used it.
- ★ **Nominee** — the one who is named. Two syllables, plain surface, precise
  underneath.
- **Anaphora** — referring back to something already named.
- **Epithet** — the descriptive name attached to a person.
- **Cognomen** — the Roman name you were actually known by.
- **Locution** · **Colloquy** · **Deixis** · **Predicate** · **Lexeme**

## Astronomy

Visibility is not a metaphor here. It is the literal subject of the field.

- ★ **Magnitude** — the scale astronomers use for how bright a thing appears from
  where we stand. A visibility measurement with two thousand years of standing
  behind it.
- ★ **Parallax** — fixing a position by observing it from more than one place.
  Your sampling method stated as an elegant idea rather than a spec.
- ★ **Luminary** — a body that gives light, and a person of eminence. Warm and
  formal at once.
- **Ephemeris** — the table of where a body will be, over time. Your re-measurement
  cadence.
- **Albedo** — how much light a body reflects back.
- **Occultation** — when one body passes in front of another and hides it. What the
  competitor named instead of you is doing.
- **Zenith** · **Aperture** · **Culmination**

## Survey and navigation

- ★ **Triangulate** — take bearings from several positions to fix one point. Same
  idea as Parallax, plainer surface.
- ★ **Meridian** — the reference line everything else is measured against.
- **Landmark** — the thing you are known by and navigated to.
- **Azimuth** — the bearing to a thing from where you are.
- **Transit** — the surveyor's instrument, and the act of crossing into view.
- **Benchmark** — originally the surveyor's mark cut into stone.
- **Cardinal** — the four points everything orients to.
- **Waypoint** · **Rhumb** · **Trig** · **Fix**

## Standing, presence, name

- ★ **Numen** — the presence or will felt to inhabit a place. Sits one letter from
  both name and number, which is a gift.
- ★ **Ordinal** — the rank words. First, second, third. The shortlist, named
  precisely and without a whisper of methodology.
- **Eminence** — the state of being conspicuous and respected.
- **Countenance** — a face, and to regard favourably.
- **Aspect** — how a thing appears from a given position.
- **Bearing** — how a person carries themselves, and a navigational reading.
- **Tenor** — the general sense of what was said.
- **Register** — whether a thing registers. Also musical range, also the official
  record. Three good readings, no bad ones.

## Evidence and record

For the honesty posture, which is still the strongest thing you have.

- **Precedent** — what has been established and can be relied on.
- **Warrant** — the grounds that justify a belief. The epistemology sense, not the
  police one.
- **Testament** · **Deposition** · **Exhibit** · **Docket** · **Prima Facie**
- **Bona Fide** — in good faith. Says the posture out loud in a language that makes
  it sound settled rather than defensive.

## Sound

- **Refrain** — the line that comes back around. What you want your client's name to
  be.
- **Resonance** — whether a thing carries.
- **Descant** — the line sung above the melody.
- **Timbre** · **Motif** · **Reprise** · **Overtone**

## If you want my three

**Concordance**, **Magnitude**, **Referent**.

Concordance because it is the product, named by scholars centuries before the
product existed, and nobody in your category has thought of it. Magnitude because
visibility is the literal subject and it sounds like it matters. Referent because
it is the quietest and the most exact: your business is the thing the name is
supposed to point to, and you measure whether it still does.

If none of these land either, the useful thing to tell me is not what is wrong with
them but which one you disliked least. Four rounds in, that will move faster than
another adjective.
